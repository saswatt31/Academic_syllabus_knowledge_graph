CS302 Database Systems — Course Syllabus

Instructor: Dr. A. Kulkarni
Credits: 3
Semester: Spring 2027

Course Description:
Relational database design and usage. Entity-relationship modelling, SQL,
normalization, transactions, indexing and an introduction to NoSQL stores.

Prerequisites:
Requires CS101 Introduction to Programming.

Topics Covered:
- Week 1: Entity-relationship modelling
- Week 2: Relational model and relational algebra
- Week 3: SQL queries, joins and aggregation
- Week 4: Normalization and functional dependencies
- Week 5: Indexing and B-trees
- Week 6: Transactions and ACID properties
- Week 7: Concurrency control and recovery
- Week 8: Introduction to NoSQL and document stores

Learning Outcomes:
LO1. Design normalized relational schemas from requirements.
LO2. Write complex SQL queries with joins and aggregation.
LO3. Explain transaction properties and concurrency control.
LO4. Compare relational and NoSQL storage models.
