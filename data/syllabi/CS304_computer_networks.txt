CS304 Computer Networks — Course Syllabus

Instructor: Dr. K. Das
Credits: 3
Semester: Autumn 2026

Course Description:
Computer network architecture and protocols. Layered models, TCP/IP, routing,
transport protocols and network security basics.

Prerequisites:
Prerequisite: CS303 Operating Systems.

Topics Covered:
- Week 1: Network architecture and layered models (OSI, TCP/IP)
- Week 2: Physical layer and data link protocols
- Week 3: MAC protocols and Ethernet
- Week 4: IP addressing and subnetting
- Week 5: Routing algorithms (distance vector, link state)
- Week 6: Transport layer, UDP and TCP congestion control
- Week 7: Application layer: DNS, HTTP, email
- Week 8: Network security: cryptography basics, firewalls

Learning Outcomes:
LO1. Describe the OSI and TCP/IP layering.
LO2. Compute subnet masks and address allocations.
LO3. Compare routing algorithms.
LO4. Explain TCP congestion control mechanisms.
